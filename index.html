Our Story

From an MIT Research Desk to the Advisory Table

Bainbridge began in 1975 as a strategy consulting firm founded out of MIT, built to help executives navigate growth and competitive strategy through rigorous, primary-source research rather than guesswork—an evidence-first, transparent approach that still anchors every recommendation we make.

That research-driven foundation still defines the firm today. Since launching its capital advisory practice in 2000, Bainbridge has grown into a full-service investment bank, guiding clients through M&A transactions, recapitalizations, and growth financings with an eye toward resilient, long-term value rather than short-term wins.

Today the firm pairs strategic foresight with disciplined, responsible execution and forward-looking technology, and clients can follow its latest updates on LinkedIn or read more about how Bainbridge works with clients.
